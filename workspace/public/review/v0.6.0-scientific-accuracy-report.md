## Required classification language

- **Calculated** — output deterministically produced by the installed provider or a direct coordinate transformation of that output.
- **Dataset verified** — output compared against an identified external reference dataset with stated residuals. v0.6 does not claim that any result is Dataset verified.
- **Educational approximation** — scientifically motivated output from the simplified provider that is suitable for learning but not authoritative prediction.
- **Visual enhancement** — presentation changes such as body enlargement, distance compression, lighting, atmosphere, locator labels or camera framing. These do not change calculated orbital state.
- **Outside verified range** — a calculation requested before 1900-01-01 or after 2100-12-31. The model may remain numerically finite, but its declared release validation does not apply.

## Principal Moon phase definition

The four principal phases are solved from the geocentric ecliptic longitude difference between the Moon and Sun:

| Phase | Target longitude difference |
|---|---:|
| New Moon | 0° |
| First Quarter | 90° |
| Full Moon | 180° |
| Last Quarter | 270° |

The application also displays true three-dimensional Moon–Sun elongation. Elongation can differ slightly from exact 0° or 180° because the Moon can have non-zero ecliptic latitude. This is physically expected and is not treated as a principal-phase timing error.

## Event interpretation

### Moon phases

Classification: **Calculated** within the installed model and **Educational approximation** with respect to real-world high-precision ephemerides.

### Conjunction and opposition

Classification: **Calculated** from modelled geocentric ecliptic longitudes and **Educational approximation** for real-world event timing.

### Perihelion and aphelion

Classification: **Calculated** from the fixed Keplerian orbit used by the provider and **Educational approximation** because time-varying osculating elements and perturbations are omitted.

### Eclipse geometry

Classification: **Educational approximation**. Candidates require a New Moon or Full Moon near the modelled orbital node. They are explicitly marked `localCircumstancesAuthoritative=false`.

**Eclipse candidates are not authoritative local eclipse-contact predictions.** They do not provide certified first, second, maximum, third or fourth contact times, local visibility limits, weather, civil time standards or safety advice.

### Observer output

Altitude, azimuth, horizon visibility and local time are **Calculated** from the simplified model and observer coordinates. They remain an **Educational approximation** because precession, nutation, aberration, refraction and light-time corrections are omitted. Atmospheric scattering and light pollution controls are **Visual enhancement** only.

## Visual scale modes

### Learning Scale

- Compressed orbital spacing.
- Enlarged bodies for readability.
- Classification: **Visual enhancement**.

### Real Distance

- Linear AU spacing preserves distance ordering and relative orbital scale.
- Body sizes are safety-bounded to prevent visible overlap while remaining locatable.
- Full-system camera framing includes the outer-planet envelope.
- Focus and locator labels are required because inner objects naturally cluster in a Solar System overview.
- Classification: calculated positions plus **Visual enhancement** for body size and labels.

### Real Scale

- Uses physical radius-to-AU ratios for body size.
- Locator labels provide visibility when bodies are sub-pixel.
- Classification: calculated position and scale with locator-label **Visual enhancement**.

## Model limitations

- Fixed rounded orbital elements rather than time-varying osculating elements.
- No mutual planetary perturbations.
- No precession or nutation.
- No stellar aberration.
- No light-time correction.
- No relativistic corrections.
- Moon orbit uses fixed inclination and simplified circular mean distance.
- No authoritative topocentric eclipse circumstances.
- No automatic GPS request; manually selected or optional device location remains local.
- Not suitable for navigation, spacecraft operations, civil eclipse prediction, legal timekeeping or safety decisions.

## Dataset verification status

No external ephemeris or observational dataset is bundled in v0.6. Therefore, the report makes no **Dataset verified** claim and publishes no external average or maximum absolute event-time error. A future high-precision provider must identify its source dataset, licence, epoch, coordinate conventions, comparison sample and absolute residuals before using that classification.
