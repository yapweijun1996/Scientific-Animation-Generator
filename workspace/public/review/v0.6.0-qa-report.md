## Moon phase correction

- New Moon, First Quarter, Full Moon and Last Quarter use geocentric Moon–Sun ecliptic longitude difference targets of 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°.
- The principal-phase solver residual is below 0.001° in domain regression; the combined internal angular metric has average `7.1924627523653075e-9°` and maximum `4.526293650997104e-8°`.
- True three-dimensional elongation is retained independently and may differ from exact 0°/180° because of lunar ecliptic latitude.
- Eclipse candidates remain educational geometry and are not authoritative local eclipse-contact predictions.
