# Scientific Animation Generator v0.4.2 — Moon Pass

## Summary

v0.4.2 adds Earth’s Moon only. The Moon is implemented as a child of Earth, follows Earth around the Sun, orbits with a deterministic 27.321661-day period, and remains tidally locked so the same hemisphere generally faces Earth.

## Added

- Readable Moon orbit around Earth
- Stable tidal locking
- Procedural lunar maria and cratered rocky surface
- Low, Auto and High Moon material tiers
- High-quality bump and normal relief
- Natural phases from the existing scene lighting
- Moon focus, label and pointer selection
- Mobile Focus selector access
- Canvas 2D Moon body, orbit, focus, label and phase impression
- Standalone HTML, ZIP, save/load and PWA integration

## Compatibility

The v0.4.1 Earth, Saturn and existing planet visuals remain intact. No other moons, planets, eclipses, landing features or online dependencies were added.

## Moon assets

No binary Moon texture files were added. Lunar albedo, maria, crater height and optional normal detail are generated locally and deterministically in the application and standalone export.
